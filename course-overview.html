Course overview

About this micro-credential

This micro-credential will help you understand how character is formed through education and how its moral purposes can be made more visible in everyday practice. It is designed for teachers and school leaders seeking to connect character education with disciplinary learning, professional judgement and their particular school contexts.

As you work through the micro-credential, you will explore philosophical perspectives, research and educational policy, reflect on your own professional practice and identify opportunities to cultivate character through teaching and leadership.

This micro-credential is organised into four modules and takes approximately 75 hours to complete.

What you will learn

By the end of this micro-credential, you'll be able to:

  • Critically examine educational purposes, flourishing and the ethical dimensions of teaching.
  • Analyse how professional identity, disciplinary knowledge and everyday practice contribute to character formation.
  • Apply practical wisdom to ethically complex educational decisions in a defined professional context.

Your Reflective Journal

Throughout this micro-credential, you will be invited to reflect on ideas, research and professional practices explored in each module. These reflections will encourage you to connect your learning with your own teaching or leadership experience and consider how your understanding of character education develops over time.

Save your reflections in your Sway Reflective Journal as you progress through the micro-credential. Your final assessment will involve selecting a curated collection of your reflections and writing a synthesis that demonstrates how your understanding of character education and the moral purposes of education has developed throughout the micro-credential.
